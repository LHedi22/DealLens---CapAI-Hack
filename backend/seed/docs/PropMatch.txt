Series: Series A Round | Ask: $2,500,000 | Sector: PropTech | Geography: MENA

COMPANY OVERVIEW
PropMatch is an AI-powered real estate transaction marketplace for the MENA region, connecting property buyers, sellers, and renters with verified agents and developers through a data-driven matching engine. Unlike traditional listing portals (Bayut, Property Finder) that show all listings to all buyers, PropMatch uses behavioral AI to surface only the 5-8 listings most likely to match each buyer's actual preferences — reducing search time from weeks to days and improving agent conversion rates.

Founded: 2022 | Headquarters: Dubai, UAE (registered) | Operations: Tunis, Casablanca, Amman | Team Size: 18

PROBLEM
MENA real estate transactions are broken at multiple levels. Property portals have 2-3 million listings in the UAE alone — buyers spend an average of 67 days searching before transacting. 78% of buyer-agent meetings result in no match, wasting both parties' time. Agents spend 14 hours per week responding to unqualified inquiries. Developers have no direct channel to pre-qualified buyers beyond portal advertising. Transaction fraud — fake listings, ghost agencies — costs MENA buyers an estimated $380M annually.

SOLUTION
PropMatch delivers:
- AI matching engine: buyer completes a 6-minute preference profile; our algorithm matches them to the top 5-8 listings from a verified inventory database of 85,000 properties
- Verified listing and agent certification: all listings verified by PropMatch field team before publication; agents complete a PropMatch certification process (background check + training)
- Buyer-agent introduction: PropMatch matches buyers with the single best agent for their profile, reducing mis-matched meetings
- Developer direct channel: developers list new development projects and receive pre-qualified buyer leads
- Transaction support: PropMatch escrow service for secure deposit handling (UAE regulatory license applied for)
