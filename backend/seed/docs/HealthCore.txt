HEALTHCORE — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $1,200,000 | Sector: HealthTech | Geography: North America

COMPANY OVERVIEW
HealthCore is a patient management and clinic analytics SaaS platform designed for independent clinics and outpatient medical practices in the United States and Canada. We replace paper-based and fragmented EHR systems with a unified platform that manages appointment scheduling, clinical documentation, billing, and population health analytics — all HIPAA-compliant and designed for clinics with 2-20 providers.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Toronto, Canada | Team Size: 9

PROBLEM
74% of independent clinics in North America still use paper-based or first-generation EHR systems (AMA 2023 report). These systems produce administrative overhead of 15+ hours per provider per week. 42% of clinic revenue leakage occurs at the billing stage due to manual coding errors. Small clinics are locked out of the enterprise solutions used by hospital networks (Epic, Cerner) due to price and implementation complexity.

SOLUTION
HealthCore delivers:
- Patient scheduling: online booking with automated reminders, reducing no-shows by 34% in pilot clinics
- Clinical documentation: structured SOAP note templates with AI-assisted clinical coding suggestions
- Billing integration: automated ICD-10 coding checks and insurance claim submission
- Population health dashboard: clinic operators see chronic disease management trends, vaccination rates, and follow-up gaps across their patient panel
- Patient portal: secure messaging, lab result delivery, prescription renewal requests (HIPAA-compliant)
