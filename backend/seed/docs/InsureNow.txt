INSURENOW — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $750,000 | Sector: InsurTech | Geography: Europe

COMPANY OVERVIEW
InsureNow is an embedded insurance distribution platform for European gig economy platforms and HR SaaS providers. We enable platforms to offer white-labeled micro-insurance products (accident, income protection, equipment) to their end users — gig workers, freelancers, and SME employees — through a single API integration. Insurance is activated in seconds, billed monthly, and cancellable at any time.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Amsterdam, Netherlands | Team Size: 8

PROBLEM
32 million gig workers in Europe have no employer-provided insurance coverage. Existing insurance products require lengthy applications, medical checks, and annual commitments — completely unsuitable for workers with variable income and intermittent employment. Meanwhile, platforms that employ gig workers (delivery apps, freelance marketplaces, HR tools) have no convenient way to offer insurance as a value-added service. 67% of gig workers say access to portable insurance would make them more loyal to their platform (Eurofound 2023).

SOLUTION
InsureNow delivers:
- Insurance API: single API endpoint that platforms integrate to offer micro-insurance to their users
- Policy engine: instant policy issuance, real-time activation and deactivation, automated claims triggers
- Insurance products: AccidentCover (€8/month), IncomeGuard (€22/month), EquipShield (€12/month) — all underwritten by established insurance partners
- White-label UI: fully branded insurance module in the platform's own app
- Claims management: digital claims portal with 72-hour settlement commitment
