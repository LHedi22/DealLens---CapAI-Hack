GAMELAB — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $1,400,000 | Sector: Gaming | Geography: Europe

COMPANY OVERVIEW
GameLab is a browser-based multiplayer gaming platform for indie game developers targeting the European casual gaming market. We provide indie developers with a publishing platform, player matchmaking infrastructure, and a subscription-based player membership (GameLab Pass) that unlocks access to 180+ independent titles for €9.99/month — a "Netflix for indie games" model accessible directly in-browser with no download required.

Founded: 2022 | Headquarters: Warsaw, Poland | Team Size: 16

PROBLEM
184,000 indie games are released globally per year (Steam 2023 data), but over 90% earn less than $1,000 in lifetime revenue. Discoverability on Steam is dominated by AAA publishers with marketing budgets. Indie developers have no viable distribution alternative that gives them access to a paying subscriber base. Meanwhile, casual gamers in Europe (an estimated 140 million people) play free-to-play mobile games heavily monetized through in-app purchases and advertising — but many dislike the manipulative monetization and prefer a subscription model.

SOLUTION
GameLab delivers:
- Publisher portal: indie developers submit their browser-based game for curation review; accepted games enter the GameLab library
- GameLab Pass: subscriber gets unlimited access to 180+ curated indie titles for €9.99/month
- Revenue share: developers earn 30% of subscriber revenue proportional to time played on their title
- Matchmaking: real-time player matching for multiplayer titles using GameLab's shared infrastructure
- Analytics for developers: play time, session length, completion rate, and player demographics per title
- No-download architecture: all games run in WebGL/HTML5 in-browser — no app store dependence

ESG PROFILE
Data and user protection: GameLab collects player behavioral data including play time, session patterns, in-game behavior, and payment data. This data is used to improve game recommendations and developer analytics. GameLab does not currently publish a formal privacy policy on its website — this is in active development and will be published before Q1 2025. We are GDPR registered (Polish DPA registration confirmed). User data is not sold to third parties. We do not use behavioral data for advertising purposes.
Player protection: GameLab Pass uses a flat subscription model with no in-game purchases, loot boxes, or random reward mechanics — a deliberate design choice to avoid manipulative monetization practices common in free-to-play gaming. We believe this is both ethically correct and commercially differentiated.
Social: GameLab provides a fair revenue share (30%) to indie developers — significantly more transparent than opaque Steam algorithms. We are enabling small developer teams, including solo developers and Eastern European studios, to earn sustainable income.
Environmental: Cloud-hosted platform on European data centers (OVH France, carbon-neutral commitment). Browser-based architecture eliminates the need for users to purchase dedicated gaming hardware. We estimate our environmental footprint is significantly lower per gaming hour than console or PC gaming.
