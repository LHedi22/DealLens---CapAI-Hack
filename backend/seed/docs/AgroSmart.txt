AGROSMART — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $900,000 | Sector: AgriTech | Geography: MENA

COMPANY OVERVIEW
AgroSmart is a precision agriculture SaaS and IoT platform for smallholder and medium-scale farmers in North Africa and the Levant. We combine low-cost soil sensors, satellite imagery, and AI-driven crop advisory to help farmers increase yield by 18-31% while reducing water and fertilizer usage. Our B2B model sells to agricultural cooperatives, irrigation districts, and agribusiness distributors who serve thousands of farmers.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Sfax, Tunisia | Team Size: 11

PROBLEM
MENA agriculture faces a perfect storm: water scarcity (region holds 1% of global freshwater, hosts 6% of global population), soil degradation from over-fertilization, and a lack of agronomic advisory reaching smallholder farmers. Average crop yields in Tunisia and Morocco are 40-60% below European benchmarks for identical crops. Farmers waste an estimated 35% of irrigation water due to lack of soil moisture data. Agricultural advisory services from government extension workers reach fewer than 12% of smallholders.

SOLUTION
AgroSmart delivers:
- Soil sensor network: low-cost wireless soil moisture, temperature, and salinity sensors (AgroSmart-S1, priced at 180 TND each)
- Satellite crop monitoring: weekly NDVI and thermal stress maps per field, processed from Sentinel-2 imagery (free)
- AI crop advisory: personalized irrigation and fertilization recommendations sent via SMS and mobile app in Arabic and French
- Cooperative dashboard: cooperative managers see portfolio-level water usage and yield tracking across member farms
- Marketplace integration: farmers can order inputs (seeds, fertilizer) at cooperative prices through the platform

ESG PROFILE

Our platform directly reduces water usage (22% average), reduces fertilizer overuse (14% reduction in first season across client base), and helps prevent soil salinization — the primary cause of land degradation in Tunisia. We track and publish water-saved and chemical-reduction metrics per cooperative annually. Our sensor manufacturing uses no hazardous materials. We use Sentinel-2 satellite data (no carbon cost) and cloud hosting powered by OVH's renewable energy infrastructure.

Our SMS advisory service is designed to reach illiterate farmers through voice note integration. We work with the FAO Tunisia team to align our metrics with SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 6 (Clean Water).
