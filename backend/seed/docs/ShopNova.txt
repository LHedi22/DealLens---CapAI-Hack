SHOPNOVA — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $1,100,000 | Sector: E-Commerce | Geography: Asia (Southeast Asia)

COMPANY OVERVIEW
ShopNova is a social commerce marketplace for independent fashion, lifestyle, and beauty sellers in Southeast Asia, focused initially on Indonesia and Vietnam. We enable micro-entrepreneurs — primarily women running home-based businesses — to set up a shoppable social storefront, process payments, and arrange delivery through our integrated logistics partners, all from a mobile app. Think of us as the B2C layer connecting TikTok-style seller content with the transactional infrastructure of Shopee.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Jakarta, Indonesia | Team Size: 14

PROBLEM
Southeast Asia has 35 million micro-entrepreneurs selling through Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook groups — but these platforms have no native checkout. Sellers manually process orders via DM, collect payments via bank transfer, and arrange delivery through third-party couriers by phone. The average seller loses 4 hours per day to manual order management. Failed payments and delivery disputes account for 18% of all transactions. Meanwhile, large platforms (Shopee, Tokopedia) favor established brands and charge commission fees that make them unviable for micro-scale sellers.

SOLUTION
ShopNova delivers:
- Social storefront: seller creates a shoppable profile with video and image content
- Integrated checkout: buyers add to cart and pay within ShopNova without leaving the app
- Payment processing: virtual account, QRIS (Indonesian QR payment standard), and DANA/OVO wallet integration
- Logistics: ShopNova integrates with J&T Express, SiCepat, and JNE for automated shipment booking and tracking
- Seller analytics: weekly sales reports, top products, buyer return rate, follower growth
- Live shopping: real-time video stream with embedded product links and instant purchase

Social impact: ShopNova directly enables economic empowerment for 4,200 micro-entrepreneurs, 74% of whom are women in Indonesia and Vietnam — communities with historically limited access to formal commerce infrastructure. Our platform gives sellers access to payment processing and logistics that previously required bank accounts and formal business registration. We believe we are helping close the gender economic gap in Southeast Asian micro-commerce.
Environmental: Mobile-only platform. No warehousing. We use third-party logistics networks and do not operate our own delivery fleet. We have not calculated a formal environmental impact or carbon footprint at this stage.

Governance: ShopNova is managed by the co-founding team. No formal board is in place. We plan to establish a formal board including independent directors before our Series A.
