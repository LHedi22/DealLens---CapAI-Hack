COMPANY OVERVIEW
DataMind is an AI-powered business intelligence platform for mid-market companies (500-5,000 employees) that transforms raw operational data into executive-ready strategic insights — without a data science team. We connect to a company's existing data sources (CRM, ERP, marketing tools, databases), apply our proprietary analytical models, and surface the 10 most important strategic signals a CEO or CFO should act on each week, in plain English with drill-down evidence.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: San Francisco, California, USA | Team Size: 15

PROBLEM
Data has never been more abundant — but strategic clarity has never been more scarce. Mid-market companies run on 7-12 different SaaS tools, each generating siloed data. 71% of mid-market CEOs say they make major decisions based on gut feeling rather than data (Gartner 2024) — not because they don't want to use data, but because the gap between raw data and a clear recommendation requires a data science team they cannot afford. Enterprise BI tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI) require months of implementation, data engineering staff, and produce dashboards — not decisions.

SOLUTION
DataMind delivers:
- Zero-configuration connectivity: connect to Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, Shopify, Google Analytics, and 80+ other tools with OAuth in 15 minutes
- AI strategic signals: our models identify revenue risk, churn patterns, operational inefficiencies, and growth opportunities — surfaced as weekly "signals" with plain-English explanations and supporting data
- Executive narrative: DataMind generates a weekly 1-page board-ready summary of the company's most important strategic insights
- Scenario modeling: CEOs test "what if" scenarios — "what if we reduce CAC by 20%?" — and see modeled impact across connected data sources
- Collaborative decisions: signals can be assigned to team members with tracked resolution status
- API access: enterprise clients embed DataMind signals in their internal tools via REST API

ESG PROFILE
AI ethics and bias: DataMind's models analyze company operational data and surface recommendations that affect business decisions — including decisions that could affect employees (workforce planning, performance signals). We take AI model bias seriously. Dr. Timnit Gebru serves as an independent AI ethics advisor with explicit responsibility to review our models for potential discriminatory outputs. We conduct quarterly bias audits on all DataMind analytical models and publish the results internally to our board. We are developing a public AI bias audit report to be released in 2025.
Data handling: DataMind processes company-proprietary business data including revenue figures, customer data summaries, and operational metrics. This data is processed under enterprise data processing agreements with each client. DataMind's privacy policy is published at datamind.ai/privacy. We do not use client data to train models without explicit written consent (currently: no client data used in model training — models trained on synthetic datasets). SOC 2 Type II certified. All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Data residency options available (US-East or US-West AWS regions).
