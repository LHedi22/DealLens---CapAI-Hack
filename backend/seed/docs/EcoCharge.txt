Series: Pre-seed Round | Ask: $400,000 | Sector: CleanTech | Geography: Europe

COMPANY OVERVIEW
EcoCharge is developing a modular, second-life battery energy storage system for commercial and industrial buildings in Western Europe. We take lithium-ion battery packs from electric vehicles that have reached end-of-automotive-life (typically at 75-80% capacity), repackage them into grid-connected building energy storage units, and sell them as a service to commercial property owners who want to reduce peak demand charges and integrate solar generation.

Founded: 2024 | Headquarters: Amsterdam, Netherlands | Team Size: 4

PROBLEM
Commercial buildings account for 36% of EU energy consumption and 40% of CO2 emissions. Peak demand charges can account for 30-45% of a commercial building's electricity bill. Meanwhile, 3.2 million EV battery packs will reach end-of-automotive-life in Europe by 2027 (BloombergNEF 2023). These batteries, while no longer viable for vehicles, retain 75-80% of original storage capacity — enough for stationary energy storage. Today they are mostly shredded, wasting embodied energy and creating lithium mining demand for new storage systems.

SOLUTION
EcoCharge delivers:
- Second-life battery packs: EV battery modules tested, graded, and repackaged into EcoCharge Storage Units (ESUs)
- Building energy management software: EcoCharge OS monitors building load profile, solar generation, and grid pricing to optimize charge/discharge cycles
- Battery-as-a-Service: commercial clients pay monthly fee rather than capex — EcoCharge owns and maintains the hardware
- Grid services: EcoCharge aggregates building storage capacity and participates in grid balancing markets (EPEX SPOT) to generate additional revenue shared with building owners
- Installation and maintenance: EcoCharge handles installation, monitoring, and battery health tracking for the client

ESG PROFILE
Environmental: EcoCharge's entire business model is built on environmental impact. We divert EV battery packs from shredding — each 60 kWh battery pack we reuse avoids approximately 8 tonnes of CO2 equivalent from new battery manufacturing (based on Argonne National Laboratory LCA data, 2022). Our battery-as-a-service model enables commercial buildings to reduce peak grid electricity consumption by an estimated 25-40%, directly reducing carbon emissions. We are currently developing a formal carbon impact quantification methodology for each installation, which we will publish by our first commercial deployment. Our EU Horizon grant application includes a full lifecycle assessment framework.
Social: EcoCharge creates skilled technical jobs in the Netherlands. Our second-life model reduces the need for new lithium and cobalt mining — materials often extracted under poor labor conditions in DRC and Bolivia. By extending battery life, we reduce upstream social and environmental harm. We are also exploring partnerships with vocational training programs for battery technicians.
Governance: EcoCharge is currently a 4-person founding team. We operate with consensus-based decision making between co-founders. A formal governance structure will be established as part of this pre-seed fundraising process. We will appoint at least 2 independent advisors with defined roles before Series A.
Data: EcoCharge's building energy management software collects building energy consumption data. This data is used exclusively for optimization and grid services and is never sold or shared beyond the building owner and grid operator as required.
