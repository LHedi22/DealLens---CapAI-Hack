EDUFLOW — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $800,000 | Sector: EdTech | Geography: MENA

COMPANY OVERVIEW
EduFlow is a B2B SaaS learning management system (LMS) built for schools and universities in the MENA region. We replace fragmented spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and legacy portals with a single platform that handles curriculum delivery, student progress tracking, teacher collaboration, and parent engagement — all in Arabic and English.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Tunis, Tunisia | Team Size: 7

PROBLEM
55 million students in MENA are served by educational institutions still running on paper registers, email threads, and informal communication. Schools lose 30% of administrative time to manual reporting. Teachers have no visibility into student performance gaps until exam results arrive — too late to intervene.

SOLUTION
EduFlow delivers:
- Curriculum management: teachers build and publish course content in a structured format
- Real-time analytics: principals and teachers see engagement and performance data weekly
- Parent portal: automated progress reports and direct communication channel
- B2B LMS integration: university clients plug EduFlow into their existing IT stack via API
