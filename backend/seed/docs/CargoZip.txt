CARGOZIP — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Series A Round | Ask: $3,000,000 | Sector: Logistics | Geography: Europe (HQ: Warsaw, expansion: EU)

COMPANY OVERVIEW
CargoZip is a last-mile logistics marketplace connecting retailers and e-commerce brands with independent delivery drivers across Central and Eastern Europe. We operate an on-demand fleet of 4,200 independent contractors who handle same-day and next-day delivery for clients including fashion retailers, food platforms, and medical supply distributors. Our platform uses AI-powered route optimization to reduce delivery times by 23% versus traditional couriers.

Founded: 2021 | Headquarters: Warsaw, Poland | Team Size: 38 (including 4,200 gig drivers on platform)

PROBLEM
CEE e-commerce grew 35% in 2023, but courier capacity has not kept pace. Enterprise couriers (DHL, DPD) are too slow and expensive for SME e-commerce. Same-day delivery rates run 18-22 EUR per package through traditional channels. Retailers lose 14% of customers to abandoned checkout when same-day delivery is unavailable.

SOLUTION
CargoZip provides:
- On-demand delivery marketplace: retailers post delivery jobs, drivers pick up in real time
- Route optimization engine: AI clusters deliveries to minimize driver km and time
- API integration: one-click integration with WooCommerce, Shopify, and Magento
- Warehousing integration: CargoZip manages dark store pick-up coordination for 3 major Warsaw clients
- Analytics dashboard: retailers track deliveries in real time with SLA alerts
