NOVAPAY — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Pre-seed Round | Ask: $500,000 | Sector: FinTech | Geography: Europe (HQ: Berlin, target: EU)

COMPANY OVERVIEW
NovaPay is a B2B payment infrastructure platform for SMEs in Europe. We provide a single API that unifies invoicing, expense tracking, multi-currency payments, and payroll — replacing the patchwork of Stripe, IBAN transfers, and manual Excel payroll that most SMEs rely on today. Our product is built for accountants, CFOs, and founders who want real-time financial visibility without hiring a finance team.

Founded: 2024 | Headquarters: Berlin, Germany | Team Size: 5

PROBLEM
European SMEs (2.5 million businesses) are losing an average of 11 hours per week to manual financial administration. Existing solutions either serve enterprise clients (Sage, SAP) at prohibitive cost, or consumer fintech (Revolut, Wise) that lacks the B2B features SMEs need. 68% of SME finance failures are linked to cash flow mismanagement (European Banking Authority, 2023).

SOLUTION
NovaPay delivers:
- Smart invoicing: auto-generated invoices with payment status tracking and automated reminders
- Expense management: virtual card issuance with real-time categorization and budget alerts
- Payroll: automated salary processing with EU tax compliance built in for Germany, France, and Netherlands
- Cash flow forecasting: 30-day rolling cash position prediction based on invoice and payroll data
