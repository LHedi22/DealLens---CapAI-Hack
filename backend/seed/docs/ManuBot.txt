MANUBOT — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Pre-seed Round | Ask: $350,000 | Sector: Manufacturing | Geography: Asia (Southeast Asia)

COMPANY OVERVIEW
ManuBot is developing a low-cost collaborative robotic arm designed for small and medium manufacturing facilities in Southeast Asia — particularly garment, electronics assembly, and packaging factories employing 50-500 workers. Our ManuBot-1 robotic arm handles repetitive assembly tasks (component insertion, labeling, packaging) and is designed to be operated by workers with no robotics training, using a visual programming interface on a tablet.

Founded: 2024 | Headquarters: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam | Team Size: 5

PROBLEM
SME manufacturers in Southeast Asia face rising labor costs (Vietnamese manufacturing wages up 12% year-over-year) and increasing pressure from global buyers demanding quality consistency. Existing industrial robotic arms (FANUC, ABB, KUKA) cost $80,000-$250,000 per unit and require specialized engineers to program and maintain — completely inaccessible to SME factories. Large factories automate; SME factories cannot, losing competitiveness to larger regional players and Chinese manufacturers.

SOLUTION
ManuBot-1 delivers:
- Affordable collaborative robotic arm: target price $12,000-$18,000 per unit (6-10x cheaper than industrial alternatives)
- No-code programming: factory supervisors program tasks using visual drag-and-drop interface on an Android tablet, no technical training required
- Safety-first design: force-limited collaborative design (ISO 10218 compliant) — safe to operate alongside human workers without safety caging
- Cloud monitoring: remote performance dashboard for factory managers and ManuBot maintenance team
- Modular end-effectors: quick-swap grippers, suction cups, and label applicators for different task types
