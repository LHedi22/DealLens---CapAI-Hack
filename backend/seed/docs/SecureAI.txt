COMPANY OVERVIEW
SecureAI is an AI-native threat detection and response platform for mid-market enterprises (500-5,000 employees) in North America. We use large language model-powered analysis of system logs, network traffic, and endpoint telemetry to detect and explain threats in plain English — enabling security teams without dedicated threat hunters to operate at enterprise-grade protection levels. We replace the SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) systems that require armies of analysts to interpret, with a platform that surfaces real threats with context and recommended actions.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Austin, Texas, USA | Team Size: 12

PROBLEM
Cybercrime damages will exceed $10.5 trillion annually by 2025 (Cybersecurity Ventures). The problem is not tools — enterprises already have firewalls, EDR, and SIEMs. The problem is signal-to-noise ratio: security tools generate 2,000-5,000 alerts per day, and 99% are false positives. Security analyst teams in mid-market companies (typically 2-5 analysts) are overwhelmed — average alert fatigue causes 67% of real threats to go undetected for more than 72 hours (IBM X-Force 2024). Mid-market companies cannot afford the $500,000+ annual cost of a dedicated threat intelligence team.

SOLUTION
SecureAI delivers:
- AI threat correlation engine: ingests logs from 150+ data sources (SIEM, EDR, cloud, identity) and reduces alert volume by 94% through AI correlation
- Natural language threat explanations: each flagged incident includes an AI-generated plain-English explanation of what happened, what systems are affected, and what to do
- Automated response playbooks: for 60% of detected threat types, SecureAI can automatically isolate affected endpoints or block IPs with one-click approval
- Threat intelligence integration: enriched with MITRE ATT&CK framework, CVE database, and real-time threat feeds
- Compliance reporting: automated generation of SOC 2 and HIPAA security incident reports
