BUILDSMART — INVESTOR PITCH DECK
Series: Seed Round | Ask: $600,000 | Sector: Construction Tech | Geography: MENA

COMPANY OVERVIEW
BuildSmart is a construction project management SaaS platform designed for small and medium construction firms and real estate developers in the MENA region. We digitize project timelines, material procurement, subcontractor coordination, and site safety reporting — all in a mobile-first platform that works offline in low-connectivity construction sites.

Founded: 2023 | Headquarters: Tunis, Tunisia | Team Size: 6

PROBLEM
The MENA construction sector is a $320 billion annual market, but 91% of projects are still managed through WhatsApp groups, paper registers, and manual Excel tracking (Arab Contractors Federation estimate, 2023). Project overruns average 38% over budget and 45% over schedule in the region. Site safety incidents cost firms an estimated $1.2 billion annually in the GCC alone. Medium-sized construction firms (50-500 employees) cannot afford enterprise ERP systems costing $200,000+ to implement.

SOLUTION
BuildSmart delivers:
- Project timeline management: Gantt-style task management with milestone alerts and subcontractor assignment
- Material procurement tracker: purchase orders, supplier invoices, delivery confirmations in one flow
- Site safety reporting: digital safety checklists, incident reports with photo documentation, compliance scoring
- Subcontractor coordination: invitation-based portal for subcontractors to receive assignments and confirm completion
- Mobile-first: full Android and iOS app with offline mode — works without Wi-Fi on site
